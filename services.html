Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about timelines, pricing, SEO, support, and website management.

How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on the size of the project, but most business websites take between 7 to 14 days. For more complex systems like e-commerce or custom software, we will provide a specific timeline after our first meeting.

How much does a professional website cost?

We offer different packages based on your needs. Our goal is to provide high-quality, modern websites that are affordable for small businesses and students. Contact us for a quick quote in Kenyan Shillings.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes, 100%. Every site we build at D-LABS is mobile-first. This means it will look and work perfectly on smartphones, tablets, and computers.

Do you help with SEO and appearing on Google?

Absolutely. We build SEO (Search Engine Optimization) into the foundation of your site. We ensure your code is clean and fast so that Google can find and rank your business easily.

Can I update the website myself later?

Yes. We can build an easy-to-use admin panel where you can change text, upload new images, or post blog articles without needing to write any code.

What happens if I need help after the site is launched?

We do not just build and leave. We offer ongoing support to ensure your site stays secure and updated. You can always reach us via our WhatsApp chat for quick assistance.
