SEO Basics for Small Business Owners

How to Get Found by the Right Customers

April 18, 2026 7 min read SEO

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) sounds complicated, but it is actually quite simple. It is the art of making sure that when someone searches for a service you offer, your name comes up first.

How to start winning at SEO

Be Specific

Instead of competing for broad global keywords, focus on local relevance by positioning your business as a trusted web development partner in Embu. This makes it easier to win local searches.

Answer Questions

Think about what your customers ask you every day. Write short articles, like this one, answering those questions. Google loves helpful content.

Google My Business

Make sure your business is on the map. This helps local customers find your physical location or your local service area instantly.

The Result: Good SEO means you stop chasing customers and start letting them find you.
